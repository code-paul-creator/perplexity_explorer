📐 Section 7 — Entropy, bits, and perplexity NEW

Simulated

Perplexity = 2H where H is entropy in bits. Play with probabilities to see how they relate.

Key formulas:
Entropy: H = −Σ P(wᵢ) · log₂ P(wᵢ)
Perplexity: PP = exp(average of −ln P) = 2H
